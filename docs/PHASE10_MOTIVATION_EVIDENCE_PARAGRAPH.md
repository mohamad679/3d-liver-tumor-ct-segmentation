# Motivation-Letter Evidence Paragraph

ProtoEM-CT demonstrates my ability to carry a biomedical machine-learning project through rigorous scientific closure rather than stopping at favorable-looking prototypes. I built a leakage-controlled CT tumor-segmentation workflow with patient-level development splits, external-validation preregistration, prediction locking, artifact inventories, reproducible tables and figures, and a final report generated from machine-readable evidence. The project also required transparent negative-result communication: the external 3D-IRCADb-01 validation result was poor, including macro tumor Dice `0.0141157` with 95% CI `[0.00434155, 0.025013]`, and I preserved that conclusion without claiming clinical readiness or strong external generalization. This work shows disciplined research engineering, reproducibility, and the judgment to report limitations clearly.
